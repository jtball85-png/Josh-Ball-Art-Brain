# Board Meeting Minutes — 2026-W30

**Board Meeting — 2026-W30, synthesized 2026-07-24**

Five items came before the CEO this cycle, alongside routine escalation triage.

**Neptune's Garden giclée listings (ESC-013).** Storefront's audit found the five draft giclée SKUs — the entirety of the charter's #2 revenue priority — still unpublished, with an open question on whether a 5×7 size (outside the standard 8×10/11×14/16×20 set) belongs in the lineup. The board recommended the CEO confirm the blocker and, if ready, publish. The CEO rejected this, noting the company should hold off on committing specific saleable items until the Josh Ball Art charter and brand direction are more settled. This reads as a deliberate pause on locking in commerce specifics — not a verdict on the listings' readiness or the 5×7 question, both of which remain open — while the broader identity work continues. The listings stay in draft.

**POD mug/enamel-cup margin review (ESC-014).** Storefront flagged that published third-party Printful base costs put the $9.00 mug and $14.00 enamel cup price anywhere from comfortably above to well under the charter's 30% margin floor, and proposed obtaining real COGS with a reprice to ~$12–13/~$18 if the floor is breached. The CEO approved this as recommended — storefront is to get actual costs and execute the reprice if warranted.

**Outdoor workshop permit and insurance budget (ESC-015).** Market_intel priced out the real cost and ~30-business-day lead time for a Ventura beach/park permit plus liability coverage, flagged as urgent given the seasonal window. The CEO rejected authorizing budget now, stating plainly that the company isn't ready for workshop details yet. This is a explicit "not now" — the seasonal window may close as a consequence, but the CEO judged that an acceptable cost against not being ready to commit. No revisit trigger was set; this returns to the CEO's queue only if raised again.

**printful.create_product capability demotion (ESC-009, ESC-010).** The executor's automatic safety demotion (supervised → dry_run) following two rollbacks was presented as a routine ratification, no CEO action strictly required. The CEO approved.

**Closing stale Minivan Dads escalations (ESC-002, 004, 005, 006, 007, 008, 011, 012).** All eight concern trademark/handle/domain/pricing work under the now-parked Minivan Dads identity, superseded by the 2026-07-21 pivot. The CEO approved closing them administratively as moot; they remain in the historical record and would only reopen if Minivan Dads is ever revived.

No cross-department tension rose to real boardroom debate this cycle — market_intel's giclée pricing data, creative's edition strategy, and storefront's stalled Neptune's Garden drafts all point at the same unresolved giclée-launch question, and market_intel's permit research pairs cleanly with creative's workshop one-pagers — but with two of today's rulings (giclée listings, workshop budget) both landing on "not yet," neither convergence produced an actionable decision this week. Finance's continued dormancy was again noted as a growing gap, now touching two live pricing questions, but no new directive change resulted from that observation this cycle.
