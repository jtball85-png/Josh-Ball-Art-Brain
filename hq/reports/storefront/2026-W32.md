4. **Drinkware collection exists and is named correctly, but its description may be cut short.** A live check of the storefront's collections index shows a "Drinkware" collection with copy that reads, in the indexed snippet, "Josh Ball Art's drinkware collection brings... Flagging as a possible copy-quality issue worth a human eyeballing the live collection page directly; I'm not proposing a rewrite blind. Source: joshballart.com/collections.

5. **CEO-requested drinkware margin review — Bodysurf Fin Mug and Logo Mug both still sit at $9.00–$13.00 across 11oz/15oz/20oz, likely still under the 30% margin floor.** Per the 2026-07-23 audit (still the best cost data on hand — Printful base costs for this mug blank were pulled directly then), per-combo costs range $6.95–$8.50 depending on size/color, and 21 of the combos landed at 23–37% margin, most under the 30% floor. Recomputing against worst-case cost per size band confirms the floor is at risk at current pricing: an 11oz combo at the high end of its cost range ($7.50) needs a retail price of at least $10.71 to clear 30%; today's $9.00 base price doesn't get there for those combos. Independent published estimates continue to support this cost range — "Mugs (11 oz White Glossy Mug): Starting at $5.95" at the low end, "Standard 11oz White Mug: Typically starts around $8.25 plus shipping" at the high end. I'm proposing a size ladder below (Finding 5 continued in Proposed changes) that clears the floor comfortably even at worst-case cost per size.

6. **Tumbler margin confirmed still broken.** The Sunsets & Sips Tumbler remains at $19.00 against a recorded Printful cost of $17.29 — a ~9% margin, nowhere near the 30% floor. A comparable Printful-manufactured 12oz wine tumbler on another storefront retails at "$20.95" — useful market context, though that single data point could itself be underpriced and isn't authoritative on its own. I'm proposing $26.00 (see Proposed changes), which clears the floor with room (~33.5% margin) without pushing far past the market comparable.

7. **Enamel cup fix from ESC-016 confirmed applied.** Per the CEO's decision log, the enamel cup was repriced $14.00 → $19.50 (36% margin) on 2026-08-03. Current catalog sync confirms $19.50 is live. No further action needed on this SKU.

8. **Prior week's four "Josh Ball Art" brand-consistency renames (mug, beanie, two posters) still have not landed.** All four titles in this week's sync remain exactly as they were last week, missing the "Josh Ball Art" styling: Bodysurf Fin Mug, Cuffed Beanie, unframed Poster, framed Poster. These are re-proposed below as they're still needed and were presumably left as previewed/dry-run pending capability or CEO attention — not a new problem, just still open.

9. **Jacquard sold-out hygiene still unexecutable from available data.** The catalog sync continues to report only `active`/`draft` status, no stock-level or sold-out flag per SKU. I cannot verify the standing "36+ sold out" figure from the original 2026-07-21 audit. Recommend the sync be extended to include inventory counts so standing order 4 can actually be executed — this is the same gap flagged last week, unresolved.
