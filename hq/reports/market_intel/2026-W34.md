**3. Permits/insurance groundwork: solid new concrete detail on City and County process and cost for outdoor/park workshops.** Ventura County Parks requires a Use Permit for large groups or parties that are open to the public, or will have vendor booths selling goods or services, or will feature amplified sound, or will have a Jolly Jumper, clown, etc. For commercial photography specifically, permits for professional photography are $190 per day, per location, while photography for multi-day, multi-location or with props will require a special use permit and a certificate of insurance naming the County as an additional insured — this is a concrete, quotable liability-insurance requirement relevant to any County-park forage-and-print session. Separately, the City of Ventura's process is broader: the special event permitting process is managed by the Parks and Recreation Department and follows the City's Municipal Code Title 20, Chapter 20.20 and Title 18, Chapter 18.250, and your application will be reviewed by the City's Fire, Police, Public Works, Human Resources, and Parks and Recreation Department. This is research only — no conclusion on what specifically our workshop format would require, but gives the CEO/counsel a concrete starting cost ($190/day baseline) and the insurance-naming requirement to expect.
