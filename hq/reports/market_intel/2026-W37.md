**6. Permits/insurance groundwork: found a Ventura-specific insurance requirement not previously logged, plus statewide benchmarks for coverage levels.** The City of Ventura's own Film & Photo Permits page states: "A Certificate of Insurance and endorsement page naming the City of Ventura as an additional insured will be required prior to the release of any commercial film and photography permits." For a rough coverage benchmark, California State Parks' statewide standard requires "$1 million general liability and b) $500,000 hired and non-owned auto-mobile liability coverage," with "the certificate must name the State of California as additionally insured" — useful as an order-of-magnitude figure if the CEO ever wants a quote for a workshop liability policy, though this is not Ventura-specific and not a legal conclusion.
