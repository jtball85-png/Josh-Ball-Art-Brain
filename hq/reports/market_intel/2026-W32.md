**2. New concrete data for the permits/insurance research line: Ventura-area park/beach commercial-use permits and standard short-term artist liability insurance pricing are now on file.** On permits: Ventura County Parks charges "Permits for professional photography are $190 per day, per location", and "Photography for multi-day, multi-location or with props will require a special use permit and a certificate of insurance naming the County as an additional insured" — directly relevant if a forage-and-print outdoor workshop uses props (printing frames, trays, fabric) on county park land. The City of Ventura's process is slower-moving: "Film/photography application, along with the non-refundable application fee, should be submitted a minimum of 30 City business days before your proposed film/photography event", and "A Certificate of Insurance and endorsement page naming the City of Ventura as an additional insured will be required prior to the release of any commercial film and photography pe[rmit]" — so any City-land workshop needs ~6 weeks lead time plus proof of insurance, not a same-week booking. On insurance: short-term, event-based general liability for artists/makers is a well-established, cheap product — "The average cost of crafters' event insurance is about $100 for a one-day event... starting from just $49 per event", with standard limits of "general liability coverage with $1 million per occurrence and $2 million aggregate limits". For workshop-specific exposure (as opposed to just vending a booth), an add-on exists: "Workshop & Class Coverage (+ $10.42/month): Cover the cost of injuries or damages caused by workshops or classes you offer". This gives the CEO a concrete cost floor (~$50–$60/event, or ~$24–$35/month for annual coverage with workshop add-on) to weigh against workshop revenue before any beach/park popup is booked. I make no legality or adequacy judgment — that's for counsel — but the pricing and lead-time facts are now documented.
